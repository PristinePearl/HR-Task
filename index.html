How to Use — Admin Guide
Task flow · approvals · KPI rules · daily routine
1 · The task flow

A task is no longer Completed the moment an employee submits it. A manager must verify it first, and KPI counts only after approval — so nobody can claim work they did not do.

ADMIN
1

Assign the task

Task Assignment → pick employee, task, target date. Status: Pending.

→
EMPLOYEE
2

Does the work & submits

My Tasks → Update. Adds remarks + completed date. The real submit time is recorded automatically. Status: Awaiting Approval · no KPI yet.

→
ADMIN
3

Reviews

Tasks → Approvals. Check the remarks, timing and back-dated flag, then decide.

✓ ApproveTask becomes Completed and KPI is counted (based on the actual submission day).
↩ Return (reason required)Task goes back to the employee as Pending with a "Returned" badge and your reason. They fix it and submit again → back to step 2.
⚠ Never submittedIf the deadline passes with no submission the task shows Missed (KPI 0). A Daily task that fell on an off day or leave day is Excused.
2 · What each status means

3 · How to review & approve
  1. Open Tasks → Approvals. The purple number on the menu is how many tasks are waiting. (The Task Status page also has an Awaiting Approval card.)
  2. Oldest submissions are listed first. Use the Employee and Flag filters (Back-dated / Late) to focus.
  3. Read each row: Selected Date (what the employee picked), Submitted (real time, recorded by the system), Timing (on time / late), Provisional KPI and the Employee Remarks. Click a row to open the full task, including comments.
  4. If the work is fine, click ✓ Approve. KPI is now counted.
  5. If something is missing or wrong, click ↩ Return, type what must be fixed, and confirm. The employee sees your reason on the task.
  6. For many clean tasks at once, tick the checkboxes and press ✓ Approve Selected.
  7. Approve / Return also work from the Task Status page (✓ and ↩ buttons) and from the task detail pop-up.
💡 Back-dated means the date the employee picked is earlier than the day they actually submitted — for example, picking yesterday's date while submitting today. This is the main sign of someone trying to look on-time, so verify those before approving. Late submissions are never hidden: KPI always uses the actual submission day.
4 · KPI rules
SituationKPI
Submitted on or before the target date (extended automatically if the target falls on a weekly off, holiday or the employee's leave)100
Submitted late−10 for every working day late (90, 80, 70 … minimum 0). Off days, holidays and leave days are not counted as late.
Deadline over, nothing approved0 (Missed)
  • KPI is based on the actual submission day — not the date the employee selected, and not the day you approve. A slow review never costs the employee points.
  • Example: target Wednesday, submitted Friday (2 working days late) → provisional 80. You approve on Monday → KPI stays 80.
  • If you return a task and the employee resubmits, the new submission day is used.
  • Tasks completed before the approval step was introduced are treated as already approved — their KPI does not change.
5 · Suggested daily routine
  1. Open the app and press ↻ Sync to load the latest data.
  2. Go to Approvals and clear the queue (approve / return).
  3. Check Task Status for Missed and overdue tasks; use Reminders to nudge employees.
  4. Assign new tasks in Task Assignment (or let recurring Daily / Weekly / Monthly tasks create themselves).
  5. Weekly: look at Deviations and Reports & Analytics; monthly: rate Competencies and review PMS Reports.

7 · Questions & answers
An employee says they submitted but I don't see it.

Press ↻ Sync and check the Approvals page. If it is not there, the submission did not reach the server (the employee would have seen "DB sync failed"). Ask them to submit again.

Does my review delay hurt the employee's KPI?

No. KPI uses the actual submission day, so approving a day or a week later gives the same score.

What does the employee see when I return a task?

The task goes back to Pending with a Returned badge, and the task details show your reason, your name and the time. They fix the work and submit again.

A returned task already shows Missed — why?

If the deadline has passed by the time it is returned, the task is open and overdue again, so it shows Missed (KPI 0) until the employee resubmits. After you approve the new submission, KPI is calculated with the late-day deduction from the new submission day.

I approved a task by mistake.

There is no undo button. In the Google Sheet, open the TASK sheet, change that row's APPROVAL cell back to Submitted, then run recalcKPI and Sync. The task returns to Awaiting Approval.

Can employees approve their own work?

No. Approve and Return are admin-only, and the server rejects them from any other account.

How do I pause a repeating task and resume it?

Tasks → Task Assignment → scroll to Auto-Repeat Tasks. Click ⏸ Pause on the series. Pick a Resume on date if you want it to restart by itself (for example while someone is on a long leave), or leave it empty to stay paused until you click ▶ Resume. While paused no new tasks are created; tasks already assigned are untouched and missed days are not back-filled. Resume creates today's task straight away if one is due.

What about tasks completed before this feature?

They are treated as approved and keep their existing KPI.

Where can I see who approved or returned a task?

Open the task (click its row): the approval banner shows the reviewer and time. The same is recorded in the Activity Log and in the exported CSV.
